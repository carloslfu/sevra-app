# Exact condition transition change inputs

This E4 extension follows [[records/specs/inventory-condition-consumers]] and [[records/specs/automation-causality]]. It retains exact scope-relevant changes between complete condition observations. The later bounded condition-origins inspector now resolves historical business producers from complete windows; independent roots and actual dispatch remain separate. It is not a minimal cause set, a complete list of unchanged numeric contributors, an independent-business-root decision or execution authority.

## Meaning and UX

A condition transition must explain which accepted business versions changed or which retained facts became effective. Actor labels, current heads, a mutable source file and a broad lineage graph do not establish that. Keep immutable event/version/time coordinates next to each complete observation, including before and after versions when a correction withdraws an old inventory effect. An unrelated subject, estimate, draft or generic record with no prior/current effect in the tracked subject is not a relevant input.

A same-quantity accepted revision remains a relevant scope change, though it need not change truth. Basis replacement/removal retains the exact basis event, even when the same Source ID is reused with different quantity/time. An effective-time transition retains the exact due effect or opening-basis version and original introducing event plus normalized activation instant. Rebuilds are computation over retained state, not invented new business changes. Previous/current observation coordinates still support independent numeric reconstruction through the historical oracle.

Show a compact change-input summary on an observation and page the detailed inputs on demand. Do not inline a large set in status or candidate queues. Explain unknown intervals or an over-limit set honestly; never convert them into an empty independent origin or silently enable execution. Saving, reading, copying, dismissing and reconstructing remain inert. Native conversation/rendering and actual trigger-to-workflow integration follow separately.

## Capture and observation windows

Schema 26 adds two captured control families in the existing ledger: condition_pending_inputs for the current unfinished atomic batch/clock group, and condition_observation_inputs for completed observation inputs. No second history ledger or schema migration. Root/admission state remains unchanged.

Record a relevant settled-version event when its before or after exact accepted actual inventory effect belongs to the consumer subject, including cross-scope withdrawal and rejection. Both before/after version IDs remain bound to the exact immutable event. Record every scoped basis-change event. Clock inputs bind a retained current exact effect/basis to its introducing business event and its normalized eligibility instant. No actor inference or current-head lookup. Bootstrap replay through the captured baseline anchor does not collect past changes as new triggers.

Pending inputs survive 100-row page boundaries and restart in the same transaction as numeric/projection/cursor work. A basis rebuild keeps the inputs from the whole preceding batch. Do not finalize or expose pending sets as complete at a page boundary. At finalization publish the observation, its exact input summary/members, candidate and completed cursor atomically, then clear pending rows/count/overflow. Queue backpressure retains the unfinished set. Cancellation or process death preserves all or none at each publication boundary.

Every complete observation links its immediate previous complete observation as well as the previous certain one. Unknown observations retain their own changes, then start a new window. This preserves a traversable interval history without copying a growing unknown interval into every later observation. A recovered transition across unknown still reports the gap and cannot claim an established automatic cause. First certain information remains a baseline and does not fire.

## Bounds and truthful coverage

Policy inventory-transition-inputs-v1 retains at most 10,000 input entries per complete observation window, matching the qualified current inventory projection bound. Each canonical entry is at most 16 KiB and has a deterministic content identity bound to the consumer. Save count/overflow in captured working state. Deduplicate repeated exact entries without charging/counting twice.

When a further distinct input exceeds the bound, latch overflow and retain the bounded prefix. Continue exact numeric batch/clock/rebuild work and preserve its immutable feed; label the complete observation's change coverage limit-exceeded. Do not label the prefix complete or eligible for automatic work. This avoids discarding a real inventory observation or blocking the consumer forever solely because its explanation exceeds the admission bound. Future dispatch must refuse incomplete coverage. This is a declared product limit, not a hardware-performance or unlimited-scale claim.

Coverage is baseline, complete, unknown-gap or limit-exceeded. It describes the change-input window only. Even complete coverage explicitly reports minimalCauseSet=false, automaticParents=not-resolved and dispatchEligibility=not-evaluated. Unknown gaps and bounds cannot be laundered through restart, a new generation, manual continuation or human attribution. Registered contribution/control provenance and independent business roots remain required before dispatch.
