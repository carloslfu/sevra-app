# Owner approves the model's summary above the confirmation line

On November 23, 2026, after the model runs for [[records/reviews/home-counter-run-2026-11-23]], the agent reported that a Home counter request now ended with only the host's line, "Updated space “Home”.", and that the model's sentence naming what it had chosen was dropped. It asked: "Show Sevra's own summary above the app's confirmation line after a saved change?" and recommended yes, "limited to cases where everything saved and nothing failed, with the app's line still shown underneath as the proof."

Carlos first asked for the question in simple terms. The agent showed the two texts side by side and said what each choice meant, including that the rule exists "because the model can describe its work wrongly, and the app's line cannot." He answered: "Yes, show it".

## The agent's reading

- **A saved or changed view keeps the model's summary,** with the host's confirmation under it. This is what lets a person iterate on a default: they have to be told what was chosen.
- **The approval covers the case that was described to him:** everything saved and nothing failed. A failure, a change waiting for approval and a cancelled action are still told by the host alone.
- **Drafts, reusable behavior and native apps were not part of the question.** The agent left them host-only, because their host lines guard claims about content the person still has to review.
