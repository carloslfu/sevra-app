# Owner asks for a steady chat while a reply runs

On November 24, 2026, after opening the app installed that day, Carlos wrote: "When a chat is going, there's a lot of layout shift, and when it is about to finish, there is a lot of layout shift as well. Help me, and also help me show a little bit more of the thoughts and examine the whole thing, the whole UI, so there's less layout shift when the message finishes, for instance, and during generation. Examine every single thing. Examine every single detail."

## The agent's reading

- **Two moments matter most:** while the reply is generated and when it finishes. Anything that moves text the person is reading is in scope: the activity row, the answer preview, the sent message, the saved reply and the chat's scrolling.
- **"A little bit more of the thoughts"** means more than the one line shown under a closed activity row, without opening it.
- **This is interface work** under the existing app and interface authorization. VoiceOver and system settings stay out of scope.
