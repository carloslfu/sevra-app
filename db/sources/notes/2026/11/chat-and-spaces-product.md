# Owner states the product: chat in, spaces out

On November 25, 2026, after an agent described Sevra mainly as supplier-quote evidence, Excel conversion and mini apps, Carlos asked:

> Is this not supposed to be like an Excel, but with dynamic UIs and dashboards, and with a local model? I mean, all of the idea would be that the whole input is through chat, and all of the output is through. There are ways to see the data, and it maintains the database. There are views and stuff like that, spaces. Isn't that the whole thing here?

He then asked whether the docs, the README and the app match that, and on being told the README and the current-product records still lead with the older evidence-first framing, he answered: "Yes, rewrite them."

The product, as he states it: a spreadsheet you run through chat, with a local model. Chat is the input. The workbook keeps and maintains the database. Spaces (Home and saved views, with tables, charts, metrics, buttons, forms and live mini apps) are how people see and use the data. Supplier quotes, Excel import and mini apps are uses of that loop, not its definition.
