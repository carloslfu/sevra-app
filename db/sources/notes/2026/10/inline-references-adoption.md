Carlos adopted the preceding reference and relationship proposal in the side
design conversation on October 27, 2026. His instruction was:

> Add this to the design and everywhere it needs to be added, and communicate it to the agent so it knows

The proposal recommended optional inline wiki-style links, voice/text linking,
backlinks and bounded agent traversal over SQLite indexes. It distinguished
explicit mentions, possible associations, business relationships and exact-version
derivations. Stable identity, unresolved ambiguity, right-pane previews, immutable
originals and portable bindings were included. Substantive AI-inferred relations
remain derived Records with evidence/status; association alone neither establishes
facts nor authorizes business changes or wider conversation access.

Implement explicit references/backlinks/traversal first; evaluate inferred
associations later. Defer a graph database, automatic ontology building, mandatory
graph-maintenance UI and full graph visualization. This adopts design, not a claim
that current code parses or indexes wiki links.
