discussions Threads rather than Tangents in the visible product. Use Conversation,
Start a thread, Back to conversation and Share summary with conversation. Threads
have descriptive titles, for example What if we raise prices? Sharing selected
findings back is explicit and preserves provenance. Chapters are optional headings
within the same conversation history, not an upfront organizing concept.

main conversation. This refines naming and UX; it does not duplicate workbook data
or change business acceptance or authority. Continue the current reliability,
extraction and packaging work without restarting the plan.
